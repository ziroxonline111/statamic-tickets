# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-20)

First release.

- Ticket types per event with price, quantity, sales end and most per order, in a Tickets tab added to your events collection.
- `{{ tickets:form }}`: a ready-made ticket form, or your own markup with the tag pair.
- Payment with Stripe Checkout, straight to your own Stripe account. Signed webhooks, bank payments that arrive later, and closing abandoned sessions.
- Tickets held during checkout so nothing is sold twice.
- QR tickets on a printable page, emailed with the codes and a calendar file.
- Check-in on a phone with the camera or by code, with its own permission for door staff.
- Control Panel: sales per event and type, orders, attendee CSV, refunds through Stripe, free tickets, dashboard widget.
